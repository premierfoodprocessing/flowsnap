Built for responsible media saving

FlowSnap checks public links through its processing service, displays available media information and creates short-lived download jobs for selected formats.

Platform availability can change, and some services may temporarily refuse access. FlowSnap does not bypass private media, protected content or platform access controls.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How does FlowSnap prepare a download?

FlowSnap analyses a public link, presents available formats and creates a short-lived, one-time download for the selected option.

Which platforms are currently available?

FlowSnap has verified public workflows for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Platform behavior can change, and YouTube may temporarily refuse access from the production server.

Will a video-only option include audio?

When a separate audio stream is available, FlowSnap automatically adds it while preparing a selected video-only format.

Can I connect a custom domain later?

Yes. You can launch on a free github.io address now and add a purchased domain later.

What content may I download?

Only download public content you own or have permission to save. FlowSnap does not bypass private media or protected access controls.
