Supported Platforms and Current Status

Last verified: August 9, 2026

FlowSnap works with authorised public-media links from several platforms. Source platforms can change their formats, access controls and server behavior without notice, so this page reports observed results rather than guaranteed availability.

TikTok

Local workflow: Verified successful.

Production workflow: Verified successful.

Latest result: The recommended 720p-or-lower selection produced good-quality, synchronized video and audio.

Known limitations: TikTok may intermittently refuse extraction or delivery requests. Some format choices can look identical, and a historical test found a format labelled with audio that produced a silent file. The latest verification did not reproduce the silent-file result.

Instagram

Local workflow: Verified successful.

Production workflow: Verified successful.

Latest result: The recommended 720p-or-lower selection produced good-quality, synchronized video and audio.

Known limitations: Instagram may expose a video-only source format. When a separate audio stream is available, FlowSnap adds it during preparation.

Facebook

Local workflow: Verified successful.

Production workflow: Verified successful.

Latest result: The recommended 720p-or-lower selection produced good-quality, synchronized video and audio.

Known limitations: Facebook may expose a video-only source format. When a separate audio stream is available, FlowSnap adds it during preparation. Higher-resolution AV1 files may not play smoothly on every device, so those options are not selected by default.

YouTube

Local workflow: Currently unavailable in the latest check.

Production workflow: Currently unavailable.

Current limitation: YouTube is temporarily refusing access during current FlowSnap extraction checks. FlowSnap reports this as a structured platform error. Further YouTube investigation is intentionally deferred while other work is completed.

What a verified result means

A verified result means a public link completed the analysis, format selection, preparation and download workflow during the stated test. It does not guarantee that every link or future platform response will work.

Responsible use

Only use FlowSnap for public content you own or have permission to save. FlowSnap does not bypass private media, digital rights management or protected access controls.

Report a changed result

If current behavior differs from this page, contact flowsnap.support@gmail.com with the platform, visible FlowSnap error and whether you used the local or production site. Do not send passwords, cookies or unauthorised private links.
